# Assignment 1
Assignment #1 I'm curious about... and I like to know...
The learning goal of Assignment #1 is to help you:

Connect personal experience with course content
Assignment #1 Activities

Take a look at your pantry, drawer, and/or refrigerator, or browse around at your favourite grocery store
Identify a processed food you like or has a significance in your everyday life
Prepare a 2 to 3 minute video of the food and share with the class
Describe the food: brand, flavour, etc. 
Describe why you pick this food: family, personal, etc. (in about 50 words) 
List 5 scientific questions you have about this food (hint: refer to page 2 of the syllabus for an overview of the course for scientific inspirations)
Upload the video, or a link to the video on to the discussion board (please upload to the board according to your last name). You will not be graded on your videographic techniques, and you should at least aim to show the food and narrate your story and questions
Please also provide your story and questions in text.
