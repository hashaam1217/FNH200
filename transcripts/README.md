## Final exam

**Friday, August 21.** Opens **5:00 am**, closes **12:00 noon** (PDT) — you must start early enough to finish.

**Individual stage** — Lockdown Browser required. Team members should start within ~5 minutes of each other.
- **46 MCQs**, including questions taken from **team projects** (posted on the [course wiki](https://wiki.ubc.ca/Course:FNH200/Projects/2026) — check after Aug 17)
- **2 short-answer questions** (bullet points acceptable; graded later)
- **100 minutes** · the score shown immediately is out of 46, MCQs only

**Team stage** — completed together, **20–25 MCQs**, **40 minutes**, no short answers.

Canvas autosaves every few seconds. If it warns your last answer wasn't saved, it was.

### Short-answer strategy (from Judy's own marking schemes, in Episode 13)

- **Declare a position.** The green-potato question gives a mark only if you say yes or no *and then* explain. No fence-sitting.
- **Name → mechanism → scenario.** For the camping-pasta question, full marks need the method named, how it works, *and* reasons tied to the actual situation (weight for a hiker, rehydration on a trail).
- **Use the lesson vocabulary.** Marks attach to specific terms: solanine, cholinesterase inhibitor, sublimation, void structure, retort pouch.

## Highest-yield facts across the course

- **pH 4.6** — the acid line. Below → high-acid → **5D**. At or above → low-acid → **12D**, the botulinum cook.
- **4 °C to 60 °C** — the Temperature Danger Zone.
- **Water activity ≠ moisture content.** Free water feeds microbes; bound water doesn't.
- **Health Canada administers** (standards, additive approvals); **CFIA enforces** (inspection, labelling).
- **Freezing** gives nutritive quality closest to fresh, raw food. **Minerals** are the most heat-stable nutrient.
- **Freezing, drying, and irradiation don't sterilize** — they stop growth or damage DNA. Only sterilization sterilizes.
- Popular concern ranks **additives** most hazardous and **microbes** least. Regulatory concern is exactly the reverse.
